Care should feel familiar, personal and dependable.

Ilé means home in Yorùbá. We're building a trusted way for Nigerian families to understand long-term conditions, find good care and stay close, whether they live in Lagos or London.

Mission and vision

Why Ilé exists, and where we're going.

Our mission

To help Nigerian families understand, manage and get the right care for long-term conditions, with clinicians they can trust, wherever the family lives.

Our vision

A Nigeria where every family can find, trust and oversee good care for the people they love, at every stage of life.

Why we exist

Good care in Nigeria is hard to find, and harder to trust from a distance.

Families arrange carers they have never met, piece together advice from forwarded messages, and hear "I'm fine" until it isn't. Ilé Circle is where that changes: a community led by clinicians, and the first step towards coordinated care at home.

Clinically ledEvery circle has a specialist lead and clinical governance.

Our standard

What we will never do.

Trust is the product. These commitments apply to every circle, every clinician and every member of our team.

Sell your informationYour personal and health information is never sold, to anyone.
Share without consentNothing about your health is shared with family or anyone else unless you agree.
Take referral feesWe don't accept payment from hospitals, labs or pharmacies for sending you to them.
Advise in publicPersonal medical questions are answered privately, never in a group.

Community guidelines

How we look after each other.

These apply in every Ilé Circle group and on this website. Full terms are in our Terms of Use.

  1. Be kind and respectfulEveryone here is living with a condition or caring for someone who is.
  2. Keep medical details privateDon't post test results, prescriptions or photos. Message our nurse team privately.
  3. Share experience, not prescriptionsNever tell another member to start, stop or change a medicine.
  4. Check before you forwardNo forwarded health claims, miracle cures or herbal remedies.
  5. No selling or hiringNo adverts, job posts or direct hiring of carers. Care requests go through Ilé.
  6. Respect privacyWhat members share stays in the Circle. No screenshots or forwarding.
  7. Admins keep the Circle safeWe may remove messages or members that break these guidelines.
  8. Your consent mattersLeave at any time and ask us to delete your details. See our Privacy Notice.
