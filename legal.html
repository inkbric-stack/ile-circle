Terms of use

2. Agreeing to these terms

By using this website or joining any Ilé Circle group, including our WhatsApp Community and groups, you agree to these terms, our Privacy Notice and the Circle guidelines. If you do not agree, please do not use Ilé Circle.

3. Who can join

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • You may join on behalf of a family member, but you must have their permission to share any information about them.
  • Health and care professionals who join as experts must hold a current licence to practise and give us details we can verify.
  • We may decline or remove any membership at our discretion, for example to protect members' safety.

4. What Ilé Circle is, and what it is not

Ilé Circle is a community that shares general health information, education and peer support. It is not a medical service, it does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it does not create a doctor–patient or nurse–patient relationship. Read the full Medical disclaimer.

Ilé Circle is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 112 or 767 in Lagos, your local emergency number elsewhere, or go to the nearest hospital.

5. Clinicians in the Circle

Clinicians who take part share general guidance in good faith, based on their professional knowledge. They cannot assess you in a group chat. Answers given privately by our nurse team are also general guidance, unless you are later booked into a separate clinical service, which will have its own terms.

6. Your behaviour

You agree to follow the Circle guidelines. In particular, you must not:

  • post another person's medical information, photos or messages without their consent;
  • tell other members to start, stop or change any medicine or treatment;
  • promote, sell or advertise products, remedies or services, or post job offers;
  • hire, or offer to work for, any member directly through the Circle;
  • post anything unlawful, abusive, discriminatory, sexually explicit, misleading or harmful;
  • impersonate anyone, including a health professional;
  • screenshot, forward or share other members' messages outside the Circle.

We may remove content, suspend or remove members, and where necessary report matters to the authorities.

7. What you post

You keep ownership of what you post. You give Ilé a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display it within the Circle and to use anonymised, non-identifying insights to improve our services. We will never publish your story, name or photo outside the Circle without your separate written consent.

8. WhatsApp and other third-party services

Our groups run on WhatsApp, which is provided by WhatsApp LLC / Meta under its own terms and privacy policy. When you join a WhatsApp group, other members of that group can see your phone number and profile name. Your use of WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp's terms, and we are not responsible for how WhatsApp operates.

9. Paid services

Joining Ilé Circle is free. Ilé also offers paid services, including care at home, specialist appointments and HMO advisory. Each paid service has its own terms, price, and cancellation and refund rights, which we explain and you accept in writing before anything is arranged or paid for. Care is delivered by qualified, vetted professionals or licensed partners, who remain responsible for their own clinical decisions.

10. Our content

The Ilé name, logo, website design and content are owned by or licensed to Ilé. You may share our public guides for personal, non-commercial use with credit to Ilé. You may not copy, sell or adapt them otherwise without our written permission.

11. Availability

We aim to keep the website and Circle available and accurate, but we do not guarantee that they will always be available, error-free or up to date. Medical knowledge changes, and content is reviewed periodically.

12. Our liability

We provide Ilé Circle with reasonable care and skill. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for any loss arising from your reliance on general information shared in the Circle, from other members' posts, or from decisions you make without consulting a qualified professional. Nothing in these terms excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or any other liability that cannot be limited by law, and nothing affects your statutory rights as a consumer, including under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 or the consumer laws of the country where you live.

13. Leaving the Circle

You can leave at any time by exiting the groups or messaging us. You can also ask us to delete your details, as explained in the Privacy Notice.

14. Changes

We may update these terms. We will post the new version here with a new date and, for important changes, tell members in the Announcements channel.

15. Law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Disputes will be handled by the courts of Lagos State, except that if you live in another country you may also have the right to bring a claim in your local courts and to rely on the mandatory consumer protections of that country. We will always try to resolve concerns informally first: please contact us at ilecircleafrica@gmail.com or +234 902 221 1116.

Medical disclaimer

Ilé Circle provides general health information and peer support for education only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it does not replace care from a qualified health professional who knows you.

  • Always ask your own doctor or a qualified professional about any medical condition or treatment.
  • Never start, stop or change a medicine because of something you read in the Circle or on this website.
  • Members' posts share personal experience. They are not checked for accuracy and may not apply to you.
  • Clinicians in the Circle share general guidance. They cannot examine you, and their answers are not a consultation.
  • Reference to any hospital, product or service is not an endorsement unless we say so clearly.
  • In an emergency, including chest pain, sudden weakness, slurred speech, severe breathing difficulty or confusion, call 112 or 767 in Lagos, or your local emergency number, or go to the nearest hospital immediately.
