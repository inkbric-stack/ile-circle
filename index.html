Questions

Common questions.

Something else? WhatsApp 0902 221 1116 or email ilecircleafrica@gmail.com.

What's free and what's paid?

Ilé Circle is free: the health circles, weekly guides and monthly live sessions. Ilé Care services are paid: care at home, specialist appointments, health insurance advice and ambulance services. We always agree the price with you before anything starts.

How do I book care for my parents?

Fill in the two-minute request form or WhatsApp 0902 221 1116. We'll call you within one working day to understand what's needed, recommend the right person and confirm the price.

Which areas do you cover?

We currently arrange care across Lagos. Tell us where your parent lives when you get in touch and we'll confirm on the call whether we can cover their area. If they live outside Lagos, still let us know: we're planning where to grow next.

I live abroad. Can I arrange and pay for care?

Yes. Ilé is built for children in the UK, US, Canada and elsewhere looking after a parent in Lagos. We keep you updated after visits and appointments. Please only arrange care with your parent's agreement.

Who provides the care?

Vetted carers and nurses, and qualified specialists such as doctors, physiotherapists and dietitians. Ilé's clinical governance is chaired by Dr Chris Anele, a consultant neurosurgeon.

Will a doctor answer questions in the free circle?

The circles share general guidance from clinicians. Personal questions go to our nurse team in a private chat, never the group. The circle does not diagnose or prescribe, and it does not replace your own doctor.

Will other members see my phone number?

In WhatsApp groups, members can see each other's numbers. Announcements come from a channel that hides numbers, so you can follow announcements only if you prefer.
